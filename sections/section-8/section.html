FRANK Expansion Engine

Owner: Emre. Our answer to Question 1 of the FRANK × Nova SBE challenge: how to automate and augment account management. One system turns usage data FRANK already has into signed expansion: signal → nudge → trial → signed.

  1. Detect: Usage Radar. Scores every account on unused flows, inactive users and offices, and requests FRANK saw in the inbox but did not execute. Compares each client with similar agencies.
  2. Augment: value report. A monthly report to the agency owner: hours saved, euro value and one next step.
  3. Convert: zero-touch upgrade. Small agencies get a one-tap 14-day trial that converts unless they opt out, with the contract amendment sent for e-signature. Large brokers get a ready-made expansion plan for the Engagement Lead.
