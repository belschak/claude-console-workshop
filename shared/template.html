From signal to signed, on its own.

  1. 1 Signal The Usage Radar scores every account each week: plan limits reached, flows left off, users gone quiet, and requests FRANK saw in the inbox but did not run. Each client is compared with agencies on the same plan.
  2. 2 Nudge The agency owner gets one clear message with their own numbers, plus a monthly value report: hours saved, what that time is worth, and one next step.
  3. 3 Trial One tap starts a 14-day trial of the flow or plan. It stays on afterwards unless the client opts out, and the offer says so up front.
  4. 4 Signed The contract amendment goes out for e-signature. Large broker groups skip the nudge: their Engagement Lead gets a ready expansion plan and the email to send.

What is real in the app, and what is not

Real
FRANK's price plans and the engine itself: detection, sizing, peer comparison, messages and routing.
Synthetic
The 150 clients and their usage, generated to match the brief. Clients have codes, not names.
Simulated
The clients' answers, the trials and the signatures.
